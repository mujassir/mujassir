Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

RunCheck shows your daily steps and your walk, jog, and run stats on iPhone, using data already in Apple Health. It is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. RunCheck has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising, and it does not send any of your data to the developer or to anyone else.

The only time RunCheck goes online is to check for updates: about once a day it asks Apple's App Store for the latest RunCheck version number, so it can tell you when an update is out. That request contains only RunCheck's App Store ID and your country code, never Health data, settings or anything that identifies you.

What RunCheck uses, and why

Data Why it's used Where it goes
Apple Health, read only (steps, walking and running distance, walking and running speed, active energy, exercise minutes, workouts) Shows your daily, weekly, and monthly steps and walking, jogging, and running stats Read on your iPhone only. RunCheck never writes to Apple Health
Settings (step goal, units, body weight, speed thresholds) Remembers your preferences and estimates energy for sessions Saved on your iPhone only

RunCheck does not use your location, camera, contacts, or microphone.

RunCheck asks for Health access when you first open it. You can change it any time in the Health app (your profile → Apps → RunCheck). The app shows whatever data you allow.

Health data

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties. RunCheck does not store health data in iCloud.

What RunCheck does not do

  • It does not collect, upload, or transmit personal data.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not use third-party SDKs, analytics, or crash reporting services.

Your data and deleting it

RunCheck keeps only your settings, on your iPhone. Deleting RunCheck removes them. Your Health data stays in the Health app, which you control.

Children

RunCheck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/mujassir/ios-fitness-app/issues.
