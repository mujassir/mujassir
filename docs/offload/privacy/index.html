Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Offload helps you free iPhone space by moving videos to cloud storage you already own. Your videos go straight from your iPhone to your cloud. There is no Offload server, and the developer never receives your videos, photos, or account details.

What Offload accesses

  • Your Photos library. With your permission, Offload reads the videos in your library to show what uses space, uploads the ones you choose, and saves restored videos back to Photos. You can change this anytime in Settings › Apps › Offload › Photos.
  • Cloud accounts you connect. You sign in on the provider’s own page (Google, Microsoft or Dropbox), and Offload asks for the narrowest access each one offers: on Google Drive it can only see files it created; on OneDrive and Dropbox it can only use its own app folder (Apps/Offload). It can’t see your other files. Offload also reads your account’s name and email to label the account, and your storage usage to show free space.

Your videos go straight to your cloud

Videos are uploaded directly from your iPhone to the cloud account you pick, in original quality and with their original metadata (such as the date and, if the video has one, its location). Each uploaded file gets a short note, “Moved from iPhone by Offload”, with its original filename.

Sign-in stays on your iPhone

Sign-in tokens are stored in this iPhone’s Keychain, marked “this device only”. They are not synced to iCloud, not included in backups, and never leave the device. Disconnecting an account deletes them.

Your library list is in your iCloud

The list of moved videos (titles, dates, sizes, durations, small thumbnails, and where each video is stored) is kept on your iPhone, in your private iCloud database that only you can access, and as a file named index.json in each connected cloud account, so it can be rebuilt on a new device. Settings stay on your iPhone and are shared only with Offload’s own widgets and its Files app location.

Intelligence runs on-device

Scene labels, suggested titles and search use Apple’s on-device Vision and Apple Intelligence models; the video frames they look at never leave your iPhone. To show place names, Offload sends a video’s location coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service, covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing

When you create a share link, your cloud provider makes that one video viewable by anyone who has the link, until you stop sharing it in Offload or in your cloud account.

Deleting videos from your iPhone

Offload only deletes a video from your iPhone if you ask it to, and only after checking that the cloud copy matches the original. iOS asks you to confirm, and keeps deleted videos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Auto-move rules never delete videos in the background.

What Offload does not do

  • It does not run an Offload backend that stores your media, and there is no Offload account.
  • It does not show ads or use analytics, tracking, or other third-party SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Services Offload works with

Your videos and library list are stored by the services you choose, under their own privacy policies: Google, Microsoft, Dropbox and Apple (iCloud).

Your choices

  • Disconnect a cloud account in Offload at any time; this deletes its sign-in tokens from your iPhone.
  • Revoke Offload’s access in your Google, Microsoft or Dropbox account settings.
  • Delete Offload’s folder in your cloud to remove the uploaded videos and library file.
  • Turn off Photos access in Settings › Apps › Offload › Photos, or delete the app.

Children

Offload does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at https://mujassir.com/offload/privacy/ with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be posted at github.com/mujassir/ios-storage-manager/issues.
