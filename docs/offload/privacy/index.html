Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Offload helps you free iPhone space by moving videos to cloud accounts you connect. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your videos go straight to your cloud

Offload uploads videos directly from your iPhone to the cloud accounts you connect. There is no Offload server, and the developer never receives your videos, photos, or account details.

Sign-in stays on your iPhone

Sign-in tokens are stored in this iPhone’s Keychain, marked “this device only”. They are not synced to iCloud, not included in backups, and never leave the device. Disconnecting an account deletes them.

Your library list is in your iCloud

The list of moved videos (titles, dates, sizes, small thumbnails) is kept in your private iCloud database, which only you can access, plus a copy in each cloud account.

Intelligence runs on-device

Scene labels, titles and search use Apple’s on-device Vision and Apple Intelligence models. Place names are looked up with Apple Maps.

Limited access

On OneDrive the app can only use its own app folder. On Google Drive it can only see files it created.

Photos access

Offload needs Photos access to scan, export, and optionally delete videos you choose to move. You can change this anytime in Settings › Apps › Offload › Photos.

What Offload does not do

  • It does not run an Offload backend that stores your media.
  • It does not show ads or use third-party analytics SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share your content with anyone.

Children

Offload does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at https://mujassir.com/offload/privacy/ with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be posted at github.com/mujassir/ios-storage-manager/issues.
