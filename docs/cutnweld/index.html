Cut. Join. Polish.

A clear timeline, live preview, and tools that stay on your device — from the first trim to a 4K export.

Timeline

Cut and join with confidence

Split, trim, duplicate and reorder clips on a clear timeline. Speed from 0.1× to 10×, reverse, freeze frames, and eight transitions keep the edit moving.

  • Photos with adjustable duration and gentle motion.
  • In, out and combo animations for clips, photos and text.
  • Autosave, plus undo and redo of up to 200 steps.

Captions

Captions that write themselves

Transcribe speech into animated captions on device. Classic, Karaoke and Bold styles with a highlight colour — edit the text, and clear fillers in one tap.

  • On-device speech recognition only — never a server.
  • Word timing for Karaoke-style highlights.
  • Opens on the caption at the playhead when you edit.

Look

Filters, enhance, cutout

Twenty filters and adjust sliders for light and colour. Auto Enhance fixes brightness, contrast and colour. Remove the background from people, or Auto Reframe so wide shots fit a vertical canvas.

  • Noise reduction for clearer voices.
  • Crop, rotate and flip.
  • 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 3:4 and 16:9 canvases.

Speed & audio

Pace and music that sit right

Dial speed from a crawl to a blur. Add your own audio or the sound from another video — music dips automatically while someone is speaking.

  • Start a song from any point, cut it, or repeat it to the end.
  • Waveforms on the timeline for precise cuts.
  • Recent tracks stay a tap away across projects.

Export

Share without a watermark

Export 720p, 1080p or 4K at 24, 30 or 60 fps in HEVC or H.264. See the file size before you export, then save to Photos or share to your favourite apps.

  • No watermark unless you add your own text or image.
  • Opening and closing slides when you want a title card.
  • Everything stays on device until you choose to share.

Built-in, not bolted on

Native Swift, Metal and Apple’s on-device frameworks — no third-party SDKs, no cloud AI, no account.

  • Auto captions
  • Music ducking
  • Auto enhance
  • Auto reframe
  • Background cutout
  • Up to 4K

Private by design

Your videos never leave your device. Editing, AI features, captions and export all happen on your iPhone or iPad. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
