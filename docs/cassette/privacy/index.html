Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Cassette is an offline music player for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Cassette does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information or usage data.

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app doesn’t need an internet connection to play music.

Update check

On iPhone and iPad, about once a day Cassette asks Apple’s App Store for the latest Cassette version number, so it can tell you when an update is out and take you to the App Store page. That request contains only Cassette’s App Store ID and your country code. It never includes your music, your library or anything that identifies you, and nothing is stored on any server of ours. If there’s no connection, the check is simply skipped.

Your music files

Cassette reads the audio files in the folders you choose, so it can show and play them. It keeps a list of those songs, their tags (such as title, artist and album), their artwork, and your playback position only on your own device. Your files are never copied off the device or uploaded anywhere.

You can remove a folder from the app at any time, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Cassette does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be posted at github.com/mujassir/ios-music-player/issues.
