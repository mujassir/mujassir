Your files, your device No account Free · iPhone & iPad

Your library. Nowhere else.

Pick folders from Files or iCloud Drive. Cassette indexes what’s inside, plays it on device, and never copies or uploads a note of it.

Listen

Now Playing

Large artwork, seek bar, shuffle and repeat — with Lock Screen and Control Center controls, including scrubbing. Audio keeps going in the background and with the screen locked.

  • Shuffle, repeat one and repeat all — picks up where you left off.
  • CarPlay: browse folders and play on your car’s screen.
  • Siri: “Play Road Trip in Cassette” or “Shuffle Cassette” — also in Shortcuts and Spotlight.

Folders

Pick your folders

Choose one or more music folders. Nested folders are found automatically. Your files stay where they are — Cassette never copies or uploads them.

  • Add folders from the Files app or iCloud Drive.
  • Rescan when you add new tracks.
  • Remove a folder anytime; deleting the app clears only Cassette’s index.

Library

Browse folder by folder

The sidebar lists every folder with songs. Open one to see artwork, titles and lengths — then tap a track to queue the rest of the folder in your current sort order.

  • Sort by name, date modified or date created.
  • Ascending or descending — remembered between launches.
  • Current song stays highlighted while you browse.

Search

Find any song

Search songs, artists and albums at once. Matching results jump you to the right folder and start playback.

  • Every word in your query can match title, artist or album.
  • Better matches rise to the top.
  • Search from the mini player when you’re already listening.

Formats you already have

MP3, M4A, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, WAV and AIFF — with title, artist, album, duration and embedded artwork from the files themselves.

  • MP3
  • M4A
  • AAC
  • ALAC
  • FLAC
  • WAV

Private by design

Your music never leaves your device. Cassette doesn’t collect any data, show ads or need an account. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
