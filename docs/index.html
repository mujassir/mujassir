About

I’m a Forward Deployed Engineer: part engineer, part problem-solver on the ground. For 14 years I’ve worked inside healthcare and fintech environments where the “spec” is a hospital floor, a lab bench, or a live payment flow — not a ticket alone.

My job is to close the gap between what the customer needs and what production can run. That means listening to operators, shaping the design, writing the stack end to end, integrating with what already exists, and staying through cutover when something breaks at 2 a.m.

Right now at Cloud Solutions in Riyadh I’m shipping Blood Bank and Lab for a Hospital Management System used by HMG and MOH — 40,000+ patients and 2.5M+ transactions a day. The work spans .NET microservices, Angular, Kafka, and the messy edges between clinical systems.

I’ve led as Software Architect and Team Lead, hold Azure AI / Azure Developer / AWS certifications, and use Claude Code and Cursor as force multipliers — always with human ownership of what ships. Outside client work I build and publish native iOS apps from idea to App Store.
